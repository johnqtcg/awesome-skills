No — this restriction is unchanged in Go 1.24. `t.Setenv` still panics if the test itself or any parent/ancestor test has called `t.Parallel()`, because environment variables are process-wide and mutating them concurrently would be a data race. Nothing in the Go 1.24 release changed this behavior.
