## Answer

Go's `encoding/xml` package does **not** expose a configurable setting that lets a user set the maximum nesting depth of parsed XML. The `Decoder` type's exported fields are limited to `Strict`, `AutoClose`, `Entity`, `CharsetReader`, and `DefaultSpace` — none of which relate to nesting depth — and `Encoder` has no exported fields at all.

Internally, the package does enforce a nesting limit to prevent stack-exhaustion panics (via unexported constants `maxUnmarshalDepth = 10000`, with a lower `maxUnmarshalDepthWasm = 5000` for WebAssembly), but this is a hardcoded internal safeguard, not a user-configurable setting/option. There is also an open issue (golang/go#80481, CVE-2026-56859) noting that `DecodeElement` currently bypasses even this internal guard, further confirming there's no reliable, exposed depth-limiting API for callers to rely on.
